# Character lines (English)

> Format: `## <character id> Name` starts a character, `### <key>` starts a group of lines, and every line starting with `- ` is one line. Other lines (like the `>` voice guides) are ignored by the script.
> `{k}` shows the character's signature kaomoji. Rules are in `docs/characters/DIALOGUE.md` section 七. Max ~40 characters per line, not counting `{k}`. No double quotes. After editing run `python3 scripts/lines2json.py`.

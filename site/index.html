Questions

Can I try it now?

Yes. While it waits for the Chrome Web Store, you can build it from the open-source code in a few minutes and load it into Chrome. How to install the beta →

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier. If you turn on AI you use your own key, and free tiers are enough. Want to chip in anyway? Buy me a coffee on Ko-fi →

Do I need an AI key?

No. A small built-in model recognises what’s playing offline. Connecting your own AI is optional, and it’s only asked when the built-in model is unsure.

Will it get in my way?

It only appears while something is actually making sound, and tucks itself away 5 minutes after everything stops. You can drag it, resize it, or hide it on specific sites.

Can I make my own character?

Yes. Any Petdex-format pet (pet.json + spritesheet.webp) can be imported, and the GitHub repo explains the character format.

Which languages does it support?

English, Japanese and Chinese. It follows your browser’s language, and you can switch any time from the top of the settings page.
